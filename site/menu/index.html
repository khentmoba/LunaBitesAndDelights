Delivery & pickup

What to expect when you order from Purok 5.

Do you deliver to Cabadbaran?
We deliver around Magallanes and nearby Cabadbaran areas. The rider confirms the delivery fee before dispatch.
How much is delivery?
The delivery fee is separate from the food and confirmed by your rider. The prices on this page are for the food only.
What are your hours?
9am to 8pm daily.
How do I pay?
Cash or GCash on delivery or pickup.
Can I pick up instead?
Yes. Choose pickup in the app and collect at Purok 5, Poblacion, Magallanes — unahan lang gamay sa 7/11.
